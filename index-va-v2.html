OUR STORY

Where movement meets culture.

Co-founders Manraj and Shahnil, military officers' sons, first met during a posting in Tezpur. Combined with corporate insights from Krishna (EY), they set out to establish a radical running community in Delhi.

"A first 5K is celebrated just as much as a marathon PR. We run together, we stay together."

DRC is tempered by pre-dawn discipline, community coffee socials, and an absolute zero-drop policy. Every Sunday at lodhi gardens or curated city loops.
