Never run
with a club?

How slow is too slow

There isn't a too slow. The 7:30+ group runs with a leader at the back, and on Sundays people walk stretches of the 5K. In three years we've never left anyone on a route.

What do I bring

Shoes, water, and your phone. Bag drop is informal — someone's car boot, usually. Skip the headphones on your first run so you can actually hear people.

Do I have to pay

No. The weekly runs are free and always will be. Ticketed events are marked on the card; merch is how we cover printing bibs and route markers.

I'll be the only one who doesn't know anyone

Everyone's first run is like that. Find whoever's holding the flag at the start, say it's your first — you'll be handed off to a pace group in about ten seconds.
