Your first run

What actually happens, in order.

Nobody's timed, nobody's tested, and nobody here is fast enough to make you feel slow on purpose.

Join the group the night before

Route pin, meeting point and pace groups go out around 9 PM. React with a tick so we know to look for you.

Find the flag at the start

Someone holds a DRC flag from 5:50. Say it's your first run — that's the whole introduction.

Pick a pace, badly

Guess slow. Every group has a leader at the back who runs at whoever is last. Dropping a group mid-run is normal.

Stay for the chai

This is the actual club. Twenty minutes at the stall is where the group stops being strangers you ran behind.

Join DRC

There's no sign-up. Be at the gate.

No fee, no form, no trial period. Get the WhatsApp link tonight, read the pin, run with us Tuesday.
