Your first run

What actually happens, in order.

Nobody's tested, nobody's timed, and nobody's fast enough to make you feel slow on purpose.

Join the group the night before

The route pin, meeting point and pace groups go out around 9 PM. Read it, react with a tick so we know to look for you.

Find the flag at the start

Someone holds a DRC flag at the meeting point from 5:50. Say it's your first run — that's all the introduction needed.

Pick a pace, badly

Guess low. Every group has a leader at the back who runs at whoever is last. Switching down mid-run is completely normal.

Stay for the chai

This is the actual club. Twenty minutes at the tea stall is where the group stops being strangers you ran behind.
