How we got started.

Military officers' sons Manraj & Shahnil met in Tezpur, then combined with EY lead Krishna to form a radical running tribe.

Co-Founders

Manraj and Shahnil met during a posting in Tezpur. Combined with corporate insights from Krishna (EY), they created DRC.

Military discipline
