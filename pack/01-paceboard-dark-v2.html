Never run
with a club?

How slow is too slow

There isn't a too slow. The easy group runs with a leader at the back, and on Sundays people walk stretches of the 5K. We've never left anyone behind.

What do I bring

Shoes, water, and your phone. Bag drop is informal — someone's car boot, usually. Skip the headphones so you can chat.

Do I have to pay

No. The weekly Sunday runs are free and always will be. Ticketed events are marked clearly; merch is how we support community printing.

Co-Founders story

Military officers' sons Manraj & Shahnil met during a posting in Tezpur. Combined with corporate EY insights from Krishna, they formed DRC.
